About Anureet Private Limited

Anureet Private Limited is a professional digital services platform dedicated to making essential government and citizen services easily accessible through a modern online ecosystem.

Our Mission

To simplify access to Digital Services, Jansewa Services, eDistrict Services, eCitizen Services, and other online services for citizens and VLE operators across India.

What We Do

  • Provide a unified platform for multiple government and digital services
  • Empower VLE operators with a dedicated dashboard
  • Ensure secure, transparent, and efficient service delivery
  • Real-time application tracking and status updates

Our Commitment

We are committed to transparency, security, and professional service delivery. Every transaction is secured with verified payment gateways, and every application is tracked with a unique ID.
